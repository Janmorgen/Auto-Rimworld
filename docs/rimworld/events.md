# Events

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

The actual incidents that fire during play. For the pacing logic behind them, see [The Storyteller](storyteller.md).

## Storyteller-Triggered Incidents

**Weather & temperature**
- **Cold snap** — outdoor temperature drops sharply (~20°C) for 1.5–3.5 days; crops start dying around −10°C, hypothermia is the main colonist risk.
- **Heat wave** — outdoor temperature rises sharply for a similar duration; risks heatstroke, cooler failures, and fires in already-hot biomes.
- **Volcanic winter** — a longer, colony-wide temperature and light drop that also halves wildlife density.
- **Toxic fallout** — a cloud of toxins blankets the map, forcing colonists indoors and threatening outdoor animals/plants.
- **Eclipse** — blocks sunlight for its duration, killing solar panel output.
- **Aurora** — a purely positive night-sky event; gives a beauty-based mood bonus to anyone outside watching, and makes skygazing far more effective as recreation.

**Electrical/structural**
- **Short circuit ("Zzztt")** — can spark fires at exposed power conduits/batteries; avoidable with hidden conduits and roofed equipment — see [Power](power.md).

**Wildlife**
- **Manhunter pack** — a group of animals turns permanently hostile.
- **Wildlife self-tame / herd migration** — a wild animal (occasionally a rare one) spontaneously joins as tamed, or herds move through.
- **Insect infestation** — hives spawn underground or in caves and can spread if unchecked.

**Raids & threats**
- **Raids** — hostile factions attack in numbers that scale with colony wealth; can be a straightforward assault, a drop-pod ambush, or a siege.
- **Mechanoid raiders** — hostile robots that feel no pain and don't use cover.
- See [Combat](combat.md) for how to handle these.

**Positive/neutral**
- **Wanderer joins / refugee arrives** — a free potential colonist.
- **Trade caravan / orbital trader** — a chance to buy or sell — see [Trading](trading.md).
- **Cargo pods** — resources fall from orbit; can crash through roofs and damage whatever's underneath.
- **Item stash / ancient danger** — a walled-off ruin discovered on the map, containing loot, ancient cryptosleep caskets, sleeping mechanoids, or insect hives — usually harmless until disturbed.
- **Quests** — a broad category of their own; see [Questing](questing.md).

## Incidental Events (Not Storyteller-Triggered)

These come from other game mechanics rather than the AI director's pacing:

- **Allied assistance** — if you're allied with a faction and enemies appear on your map, they may send friendlies to help for free, or you can call them in via comms console at a goodwill cost — see [Factions & Diplomacy](factions.md).
- **Passing travelers** — neutral or allied faction members cross the map; you can't trade with them directly but can rescue or (at a diplomatic cost) capture them.
- **Masterwork/Legendary creation notice** — a passive notification when a colonist finishes an exceptional piece of gear or art.
- **Map-generation finds** — ancient danger sites and similar ruins are placed at map creation and simply wait to be discovered, rather than being rolled by the storyteller during play.
