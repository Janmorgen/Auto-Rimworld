# Room Types

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

RimWorld auto-detects a room's role from what's inside it — there's no separate "designate as bedroom" step. A room can hold multiple roles at once: a bed plus a table in the same room counts as both a bedroom and a dining room, and gives both moodlets (as long as the colonist actually uses each function).

## Common Room Types & What Defines Them

| Room type | Triggered by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bedroom | One non-medical bed | Impressiveness-based mood buff/penalty; quality matters a lot per person |
| Barracks | Multiple non-medical beds | Same Impressiveness mechanic, shared by everyone sleeping there |
| Dining room | Any table | Mood bonus for eating somewhere impressive |
| Recreation room | TV, horseshoe pins, billiards table, chess table, etc. | Mood bonus for recreating somewhere impressive |
| Kitchen | Stove or brewery | Cleanliness reduces food-poisoning risk |
| Hospital | Medical/hospital beds | Cleanliness reduces infection risk during treatment |
| Laboratory | A research bench | No dedicated Impressiveness moodlet, but comfort still matters for the researcher |
| Barn | Animal beds/sleeping spots | Houses tame animals |
| Prison cell / barracks | Prisoner bed(s) | Prisoner mood here affects recruitment speed — see [Recruiting](recruiting.md) |
| Freezer | Must stay below 0°C (32°F) | Long-term perishable storage; purely functional, no Impressiveness effect |

## Furniture Checklist by Room

- **Bedroom/Barracks** — bed(s), plus optional dresser, end table, and art/plants to raise beauty.
- **Dining room** — a table and enough chairs for everyone eating there at once (uneaten-standing has its own small penalty).
- **Recreation room** — any mix of recreational furniture; different pawns prefer different joy categories, so variety helps more than volume.
- **Kitchen** — a stove (and/or brewery), ideally with sterile or otherwise clean flooring; located near a freezer for short hauling distance.
- **Hospital** — hospital beds, ideally sterile tile flooring, and later a vitals monitor for a further treatment bonus.
- **Laboratory** — a research bench, later a Hi-tech bench with a multi-analyzer facility attached.
- **Barn** — animal beds/sleeping spots plus a feeding trough area if not free-grazing.
- **Freezer** — insulated walls, a cooler, and enough shelving/floor space for stored food.

## Rooms Without a Direct Impressiveness Buff

Workshops, kitchens, and labs don't have their own named "impressive workshop" moodlet, but a generic **pleasant/ugly environment** thought still applies wherever a colonist happens to be, based on the beauty of their immediate surroundings — so beauty still matters even in purely functional rooms.

## Practical Layout Notes

- Rooms with frequent, urgent foot traffic (hospital, prison) are usually best placed centrally; recreation and dining areas are fine off to the side, since they're mainly used morning and evening.
- A room's floor area is a hard ceiling on how high its Impressiveness can climb — see [Room Attributes](room-attributes.md) for why bigger isn't automatically better.
- Kitchens and freezers benefit from being adjacent to minimize hauling and food-quality loss in transit.
