# Nutrition

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

The underlying need and biology. For meal-making itself, see [Food](food.md).

## The Food Need

- Every colonist and animal has a Food need that depletes steadily and must be refilled by eating; going too long without food causes **malnutrition** damage over time, which can eventually kill.
- Nutrition, not item count, is what matters — a full meal, a raw vegetable, and a chunk of meat each restore a different amount, based on their nutrition value rather than how many "items" they count as.
- Hunger rate varies by body size — small animals (rats, hares) barely eat anything daily, while elephants or megasloths consume far more.

## Raw Food

- Meat and most vegetables can be eaten raw in a pinch, but this restores less mood (an "ate raw food" penalty) and raises food-poisoning risk compared to a cooked meal.
- A few plants — strawberries, some other fruit — are designed to be eaten raw without much penalty, making them a good emergency/travel food.
- Some processed foods (like potatoes) are specifically not meant to be eaten raw and carry an extra penalty if forced to.

## Animal Diets

- **Herbivores** (most farm and pack animals) eat plants and can graze freely on grass and other live vegetation in their allowed area.
- **Omnivores** (pigs, some others) eat both plants and meat.
- **Carnivores/predators** need meat specifically — they can't be sustained on plants alone.
- **Haygrass → kibble** is the standard portable, storable feed for animals that can't graze enough on their own, especially in pens or during winter when grass is scarce.

## Malnutrition & Starvation

- Prolonged hunger triggers escalating malnutrition damage to the body, eventually becoming fatal if untreated.
- Malnutrition is treated the same way as any other health condition — feeding the pawn stops further damage, and existing damage heals over time like an ordinary injury.
- Prisoners and slaves depend entirely on colonists feeding them; a forgotten, unfed prisoner can starve exactly like anyone else.
