# Food & Nutrition

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

## The Food Need

- Every colonist (and animal) has a Food need that depletes steadily and must be refilled by eating; going too long without food causes malnutrition damage over time.
- Nutrition, not item count, is what matters — a full meal, a raw vegetable, and a chunk of meat all restore different amounts.

## Meal Tiers (Cooking work, roughly best to most basic)

- **Lavish meal** — the best quality, biggest mood bonus, uses the most ingredients including fine ones.
- **Fine meal** — a strong mood bonus at moderate ingredient cost.
- **Simple meal** — the standard meal; a small mood bonus and low food-poisoning risk when made by a competent cook in a clean kitchen.
- **Pemmican** — barely spoils, ideal for caravans and long-term stockpiling, but has a mood penalty if eaten too often. Needs the Pemmican research.
- **Packaged survival meal** — pre-made, needs no cook, never spoils; good for emergencies or trade. Needs Nutrient paste research first.
- **Nutrient paste** — produced instantly and automatically by a Nutrient Paste Dispenser straight from raw ingredients, including otherwise-inedible ones. Reliable and never runs dry if supplied, but carries a real mood penalty every time it's eaten.

## Raw Food

- Meat and most vegetables can be eaten raw in a pinch, but this restores less mood (an "ate raw food" penalty) and raises food-poisoning risk compared to a cooked meal.
- A few plants — strawberries, some other fruit — are designed to be eaten raw without much penalty.

## Food Poisoning

- Can come from undercooked/raw food, a low-skill cook, or a dirty kitchen.
- A Cooking skill of roughly 9 minimizes food-poisoning chance outright; a clean kitchen (see [Room Attributes](room-attributes.md)) further reduces it.
- A food-poisoned pawn suffers vomiting and reduced capacities for a while but usually recovers without treatment.

## Feeding Animals

- Herbivores/omnivores can graze freely on grass and other live plants in their allowed area — the simplest way to feed a pen.
- Haygrass converted into **kibble** works as a portable, storable feed for animals that can't graze enough on their own.
- Carnivorous tame animals need meat in their diet, not plants.

## Food Storage & Spoilage

- Most raw food and cooked meals spoil over time unless refrigerated; a **freezer** (a room held below 0°C/32°F) is the standard long-term solution — see [Room Types](room-types.md).
- Pemmican and packaged survival meals are the two shelf-stable exceptions, making them the standard choice for caravans and stockpiled emergency reserves.
