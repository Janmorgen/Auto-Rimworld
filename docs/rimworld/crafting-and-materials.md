# Crafting & Materials

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

## Quality System

Anything with a quality stat (weapons, armor, furniture, art) is randomly assigned one of seven tiers when crafted or built:

**Awful → Poor → Normal → Good → Excellent → Masterwork → Legendary**

- The result is rolled on a distribution centered on the crafter's skill: the average result becomes "Normal" around skill level 6, and by skill 4 there's already a 50% chance of Normal-or-better.
- **Masterwork** and **Legendary** items never spawn on enemies or in trader stock — they only come from player crafting or quest rewards (masterwork can occasionally appear as loot).
- An **Inspired Creativity** inspiration (see [Mood & Mental Breaks](mood-and-mental-breaks.md)) bumps the result two tiers higher and guarantees at least Normal — timing a high-skill pawn's inspiration well is the most reliable way to get a Legendary item.
- Higher quality means better stats across the board: more damage/armor for weapons and gear, more beauty and comfort for furniture.

## Materials

**Metals**
| Material | Notes |
|---|---|
| Steel | The baseline construction/crafting metal, needed constantly |
| Plasteel | The strongest material in the game; best sharp-damage weapons, late-game armor, ship components; mined from rare deposits, deep drilling, or scanners |
| Uranium | Dense and heavy; best blunt-damage weapons (warhammers, maces); used in some late-game turrets |
| Silver | Doubles as trade currency and a decorative/beauty material |
| Gold | Higher-value decorative/currency material than silver |

**Fabrics**
Cloth (baseline, from cotton), Devilstrand (very durable, slow to grow), Wool varieties (alpaca/sheep/bison/muffalo/megasloth — each with slightly different insulation), Synthread, Hyperweave (top-tier fabric).

**Leathers**
Vary by source animal — plainleather, lightleather, heavy fur, thrumbofur, bearskin, wolfskin, and many more, each with different armor/insulation stats. Better leathers generally come from tougher or more exotic animals.

## Production Benches (roughly in tech order)

- **Campfire / cooking stove** — meals; the stove is unlocked with Electricity.
- **Smithy** (fueled or electric) — early melee weapons and metal tiles, from Smithing.
- **Stonecutter's table** — turns rock chunks into stone blocks, from Stonecutting.
- **Tailor bench** (hand or electric) — apparel, from Complex clothing.
- **Machining table** — guns, grenades, flak armor components, from Machining.
- **Fabrication bench** — high-tech items up to power armor, from Fabrication.
- **Drug lab** — refined drugs (flake, yayo, wake-up, go-juice, penoxycyline), from Drug production.
- **Art bench / sculptor's table** — sculptures for beauty, unlocked alongside Complex furniture.

## Practical Notes

- Crafting speed scales with the relevant skill (Crafting for most goods, Cooking for meals, Artistic for sculptures); quality is separately rolled per the system above.
- Stockpile zones with item-type and quality filters keep raw materials sorted and let you reserve your best output (e.g., only Good+ weapons in the armory) automatically.
- Selling excess crafted goods to traders is one of the main sources of silver — see [Trading](trading.md).
