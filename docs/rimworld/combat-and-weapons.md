# Combat & Weapons

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

## Core Combat Mechanics

- **Ranged accuracy** depends on the shooter's Shooting skill, the weapon's own accuracy stat at that range band (close/medium/long), cover, and whether the target is moving.
- **Melee** depends on Melee skill, weapon quality, and the size/health of both fighters; you cannot fire a ranged weapon at an adjacent (melee-range) enemy.
- Pawns go **downed** before they die in most cases — this gives a window to rescue, tend, or capture them, but untreated bleeding after being downed is what actually kills most of the time.
- **Cover** (sandbags, walls, natural rock) reduces incoming accuracy significantly; fighting from behind cover while the enemy is in the open is one of the most reliable defensive advantages in the game.

## Armor & Apparel

- **Plate armor** (Medieval) — heavy, slows movement, strong protection.
- **Flak jacket/vest/pants/helmet** (Industrial) — resists bullets and explosives at a smaller movement penalty than plate.
- **Shield belt** (Spacer) — a wearable energy shield that blocks incoming ranged projectiles until its charge is depleted, but doesn't stop melee.
- Material matters as much as the item type: plasteel gives the best sharp-damage protection/damage, uranium gives the best blunt-damage protection/damage (which is why uranium is the go-to for warhammers/maces and plasteel for swords).

## Threats

- **Raids** — hostile factions attack in scaling numbers based on your colony's total wealth; can arrive on foot, via drop pod ambush, or as a siege (they build a camp and bombard with mortars).
- **Manhunter packs** — a group of animals turned permanently hostile.
- **Mechanoid raiders** — hostile robotic enemies that feel no pain and don't seek cover, making them fight very differently from human raiders.
- **Insect infestations** — hives that spawn underground or in caves and can expand if left unchecked.

## Base Defense Fundamentals

- **Chokepoints** — funnel attackers into a single approach so a small number of defenders (and turrets) can cover it.
- **Traps** — deadfall traps and IED variants (EMP, incendiary, smoke) hidden in approach corridors.
- **Killboxes** — a deliberately engineered chokepoint stacked with turrets/traps/cover, the classic min-max defense layout.
- Downed enemies can be captured as prisoners rather than left to die — see [Recruiting](recruiting.md).
