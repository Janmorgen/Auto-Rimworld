# Recruiting Colonists

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

Three main ways new pawns join a base-game colony: recruiting prisoners, wanderers/refugees, and quest rewards.

## Capturing & Recruiting Prisoners

- Downed enemies (or lone wild travelers) can be arrested and become **prisoners** instead of being killed or left behind.
- From a prisoner's info tab, set their mode to **Recruit** (or **Reduce Resistance** to just build your warden's Social skill without committing to recruiting them).
- A colonist assigned as **warden** periodically "chats" with the prisoner, lowering their **Resistance** stat. How much resistance drops per chat depends on the warden's **Social** skill and the prisoner's current mood; there's a cooldown delay between each conversation.
- Once Resistance hits 0, wardens attempt an actual recruitment roll. Success chance depends on:
  - The prisoner's individual **recruitment difficulty**, which varies by their underlying "pawn kind" — a random wanderer or low-tier raider is far easier than a hardened warrior or faction leader
  - Their current mood
  - Their faction
  - Your colony's current population (recruiting gets marginally harder as you grow)
  - Your storyteller/difficulty settings
- **Keeping a prisoner's mood up speeds this along** — a bed, prompt feeding, and a reasonably clean, temperature-controlled cell all help. See [Room Attributes](room-attributes.md).
- A prisoner set to "Reduce Resistance" only can be used purely to grind a low-Social colonist's skill, then released, sold, or executed once you're done — this is one of the only reliable ways to train Social without waiting on organic social interactions.
- Prisoners can also simply be **sold to traders** instead of recruited — see [Trading](trading.md) — or, less pleasantly, have organs harvested; see [Health & Medicine](health-and-medicine.md).

## Wanderers & Refugees

- Random events can hand you a colonist directly with no resistance-breaking required: a wanderer asking to join, a refugee fleeing pursuit, or someone found chained up needing rescue.
- These arrive with their own skills, traits, and backstory already rolled — you just accept or decline.

## Quest Rewards

- Some quests offered through the quest tab reward a new colonist outright on completion, alongside or instead of goods and reputation — see [Questing](questing.md).

## Things to Weigh Before Recruiting

- Check traits and backstory-locked work types before committing — a pyromaniac or a pawn incapable of violence changes how useful they'll actually be. See [Colonists In-Depth](colonists.md).
- More colonists raise your colony's total wealth, which raises raid size and frequency — recruiting is a genuine tradeoff, not a pure win. See [The Storyteller & Events](storyteller-and-events.md).
- A pawn's disabled work types (from traits/backstory) can't be forced no matter the priority — worth checking before you invest days breaking their resistance.
