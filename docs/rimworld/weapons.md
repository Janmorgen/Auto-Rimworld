## Weapon Stats That Matter

- **DPS** — theoretical damage per second if every shot connects; actual output is lower once accuracy at range is applied. See [Combat](combat.md) for the formula.
- **Range** — the four bands (Touch/Short/Medium/Long) a weapon has individual accuracy values for; it simply won't fire past its listed maximum.
- **Stopping power** — whether a hit staggers a human-sized target; matters for both offense and surviving a melee rush.
- **Armor penetration** — how much of a hit's damage gets through the target's armor value.
- Quality (see [Crafting](crafting.md)) scales accuracy and damage on top of the base weapon stats; material (see [Materials](materials.md)) further scales sharp/blunt damage and durability.

## Armor & Apparel

| Item | Tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plate armor | Medieval | Heavy, slows movement, strong all-round protection |
| Flak jacket / vest / pants / helmet | Industrial | Resists bullets and explosives with a smaller movement penalty than plate |
| Shield belt | Spacer | Wearable energy shield that blocks incoming ranged projectiles until its charge depletes; does nothing against melee |

Material matters as much as the item type: plasteel gives the best sharp-damage protection, uranium the best blunt-damage protection — which is why plasteel is the default choice for blades and uranium for maces/warhammers.
