# Materials

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

The resources themselves — what each material is good for, sourced from mining, farming, animals, or trade. For how material choice actually affects a finished item's stats, see [Crafting](crafting.md#the-stuff-system-material-selection).

## Metals

| Metal | Where from | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steel | Mining, deep drilling, scanners, trade | Everyday construction and crafting | The baseline metal; needed constantly |
| Plasteel | Rare surface deposits, deep drilling, scanners | Best sharp-damage weapons, late-game armor, ship parts | The strongest general-purpose material in the game |
| Uranium | Rare surface deposits, deep drilling, scanners | Best blunt-damage weapons (maces, warhammers), some turrets | Dense and heavy |
| Silver | Mining (rare), trade | Currency, and a decorative/beauty material | Doubles as the game's money |
| Gold | Mining (rare), trade | High-value decoration/currency | More valuable and more beautiful than silver, but rarer |
| Jade | Mining (rare) | Ornaments, decoration, blunt weapons | High beauty, little other purpose; mainly used in sculptures/floors |

## Building Stone

Five stone types can be found under mountains/rock outcrops (2–3 per map, chosen at world generation):

| Stone | Relative Wall HP | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Granite | Highest (~510) | The default pick for defensive walls |
| Limestone | High (~465) | Nearly as tough as granite, decent all-rounder |
| Sandstone | Moderate (~420) | Fastest to cut and build with |
| Slate | Lower | Weak for walls, but a perfectly good, unremarkable floor material |
| Marble | Lowest HP, but by far the most beautiful | The standard choice for rooms that need a mood boost, and for stone sculptures |

- All stone types share the same market value, weapon stats, and melee-blunt factor; the real differences are HP, beauty (marble only), and build speed (sandstone fastest).
- A chunk of any stone type yields a fixed number of blocks once cut at a Stonecutter's table.

## Fabrics

| Fabric | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cloth | Cotton plant | The baseline fabric, cheap and abundant |
| Wool (sheep/alpaca/bison/muffalo/dromedary/megasloth) | Shearing the matching tamed animal | Insulation varies slightly by species; alpaca wool has the best cold insulation of the common options |
| Devilstrand | Devilstrand mushroom (slow-growing, needs research) | Very durable, one of the best fabrics craftable in-house |
| Synthread | Trade or random finds only — not craftable | Spacer-tech; light, strong, insulates well |
| Hyperweave | Trade, quests, or ancient ruins only — not craftable | One of the best fabrics in the game, full stop |

## Leathers

Leather comes from butchering a tamed or hunted animal; type depends on the source species. Leathers generally protect better than furs, while furs insulate better than leathers:

| Leather | Source animal(s) |
|---|---|
| Plainleather | A variety of medium animals (deer, goats, muffalo herds, and similar) |
| Lightleather | Small animals, merged into one common category |
| Heavy fur | Megasloth |
| Thrumbofur | Thrumbo — one of the best clothing materials in the game |
| Bearskin | Grizzly/polar bear |
| Wolfskin | Wolves, wargs |
| Foxfur | Arctic, fennec, or red fox |
| Panthera fur | Cougar, panther, lynx |
| Camelhide | Dromedary |
| Bluefur | Muffalo |
| Elephant leather | Elephant |
| Rhinoceros leather | Rhinoceros |
| Pigskin | Pig |
| Chinchilla fur | Chinchilla |
| Guinea pig fur | Guinea pig |
| Dog leather | Husky, Labrador retriever, and other dogs |
| Lizardskin | Cobra, iguana, and other reptiles |
| Human leather | Humans (yes — butchering a human corpse produces this; carries a heavy mood penalty to do or wear) |
| Patchleather | Not from an animal directly — crafted by combining smaller leather scraps together |

## Other Key Materials

| Material | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Wood | Chopping trees | Early building, fuel for campfires/wood-fired generators |
| Chemfuel | Biofuel refinery (from wood or food), or found on maps | Generators, mortar shells, molotov cocktails, vehicle-style fuel needs |
| Components | Trade, or crafted at a fabrication bench from steel + resources | Advanced buildings, weapons, and machinery |
| Advanced components | Crafted (Advanced fabrication research) or trade | Top-tier gear and ship parts |
| Neutroamine | Trade, or found | Producing standard medicine and several drugs |
| Chocolate | Cocoa tree (Cocoa research) | A sellable recreation-boosting food |
