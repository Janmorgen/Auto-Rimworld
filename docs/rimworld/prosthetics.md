# Prosthetics System

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

Artificial body parts, in depth. For natural organ transplant/harvesting, see [Medicine](medicine.md#organ-transplant--harvesting); for the body model these parts slot into, see [Health](health.md).

## The Four Tiers

| Tier | Cost | Relative capability | Research | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peg leg / hook | Minimal materials | Partial function only, well below natural | None or minimal | Craftable from the start |
| Prosthetic | Steel + components | Roughly 50–85% of a natural part | Prosthetics | Craftable at a Machining Table |
| Bionic | Plasteel + advanced components | Exceeds natural performance | Later-tier research | Craftable, more resource-intensive |
| Archotech | — | Outperforms bionics outright | — | Can't be crafted at all — quests, trade, or ancient ruins only |

- Within a tier, function still scales with crafting **quality** (see [Crafting](crafting.md)) — a Poor bionic leg underperforms an Excellent one.
- Paired parts stack: two bionic legs each add their move-speed bonus, so both together add roughly double what one alone does.

## Parts by Body Region

**Limbs**
- Arm/hand: Wooden hand (lowest tier) → Prosthetic arm → Bionic arm → Archotech arm. A **Power claw** sits alongside these as a combat-capable hand replacement that doubles as a melee weapon.
- Leg/foot: Peg leg (lowest tier) → Prosthetic leg → Bionic leg → Archotech leg.

**Head**
- Eyes: Bionic eye → Archotech eye (restores/exceeds sight).
- Ears: Cochlear implant (restores hearing) → Bionic ear.
- Jaw/mouth: Denture (basic, restores eating/talking function) → Bionic jaw → Bionic tongue.
- Nose: Aesthetic nose — cosmetic only, fixes a scarred/missing nose with no functional benefit.

**Torso implants** (installed alongside, not replacing, an organ)
- **Prosthetic heart** / **Bionic heart** — full organ replacements, not add-ons.
- **Joywire** — a direct brain-reward implant; raises baseline mood but carries its own risks and reputation as a morally grey choice.
- **Painstopper** — blocks pain signals, useful for a badly-injured pawn who needs to keep working.
- **Circadian assistant** / **Circadian half-cycler** — reduce or partially replace the need for sleep.

## Installing & Removing

- Installation is a **surgery** — it needs a doctor, requires the part in stock, and rolls the same success-chance mechanics as any other operation (see [Medicine](medicine.md)). A failed installation surgery can damage or destroy the part being installed.
- **Removing** an artificial part reduces the pawn back to a missing/non-functional body part rather than restoring anything natural — you'd need a fresh transplant to get a biological part back.
- **Removing a prosthetic or bionic heart** without another heart ready to install immediately kills the patient — there's no "empty" state for a vital organ to sit in.
- Installing a part onto an already-damaged natural part (rather than a fully missing one) still requires removing what's left of the original first.

## Where Archotech Parts Come From

- Never craftable, at any tech level or research investment.
- Sources: quest rewards, trade with the right (usually exotic-goods) trader, or found in ancient ruins/ancient danger sites.
- Because supply is entirely outside player control, most colonies end up with at most a handful across a whole game — they're typically reserved for a colony's most important pawns.
