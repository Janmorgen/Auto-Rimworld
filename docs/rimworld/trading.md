# Trading

*Part of the RimWorld core-mechanics reference. [← Back to index](index.md)*

## Two Ways to Trade

**1. Visiting caravans / traveling to a settlement**
- No research required to trade with a caravan that visits your map — send a colonist (ideally high Social) to talk to the trader (marked with a question-mark icon).
- You can also send your own caravan to a friendly/neutral settlement via the world map; other settlements typically stock far more of staple goods (like steel or plasteel) than a passing caravan does.
- Caravans need to carry their own food and defense, and have a weight/capacity limit — pack animals help (see [Animals](animals.md)).

**2. Comms console + orbital trade beacon (needs Microelectronics research)**
- A **comms console** lets you contact passing **orbital trade ships** — each ship type has a different inventory focus (e.g., some specialize in art and instruments, some in drugs and medicine, some in high-tech armor).
- Only items within range of a powered **orbital trade beacon** (including your silver) are visible/tradeable to the ship — items outside beacon range don't count.
- Ships only stick around for a limited time (roughly 16 in-game hours) before leaving range.
- The same comms console can also **call in a caravan** from an allied faction on demand (costs goodwill, and is capped to about once every four days per faction).

## Prices & Social Skill

- The Social skill of whichever colonist runs the negotiation directly affects buy/sell prices — a better negotiator gets more silver for goods sold and pays less for goods bought.
- Different faction *types* have different buying preferences: tech-focused factions pay a premium for advanced components/bionics/weapons, tribal factions pay well for medicine and natural materials (textiles, wood), and more lawless factions pay well for drugs and alcohol.
- Selling prisoners or slaves is possible with certain trader types and carries its own goodwill/mood considerations — see [Recruiting](recruiting.md).

## What's Tradeable

Nearly everything with a market value: raw resources, crafted goods, apparel, weapons, art, drugs, food, and prisoners/animals, subject to what a given trader is willing to deal in.

## Practical Notes

- Building up silver reserves and calling in caravans/ships regularly is one of the most reliable ways to convert excess production (crafted goods, drugs, art) into whatever specific resource you're short on.
- Trade is also a lever on colony wealth, and therefore on raid size — see [The Storyteller & Events](storyteller-and-events.md) — so some players deliberately convert silver into non-wealth-counting consumables rather than hoarding it.
- A well-stocked, well-defended caravan is generally safer than waiting on random passing traders if you need a specific resource urgently.
