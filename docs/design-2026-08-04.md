# Five levers, one design pass

The solutions for tasks #15, #19, #22, #23, #24, designed together because they share one
principle: **measure the real thing, let the game gate what it already gates, and never act
where a colonist could act instead.** Every API claim below was verified against
`Assembly-CSharp` metadata or the def XMLs before implementation; the surprises are marked.

---

## #24 Floors (`FloorMechanicalRooms`, BasePlannerModule)

Every room ever built stood on bare soil at cleanliness −1.0. Floors are now laid **as
blueprints** — `TerrainDef : BuildableDef`, so `GenConstruct.PlaceBlueprintForBuild` takes one
directly (verified) — in exactly the three rooms where cleanliness does mechanical work:
Hospital (surgery ×0.60→×1.00, infection ×1.0→×0.5), Kitchen (food poisoning 5%→0%),
Research (speed ×0.75→×1.15).

Choices: concrete when Stonecutting is done and steel is plentiful (open flame on a flammable
floor), wood plank otherwise (no research, 3 wood/cell). Gated on `BuildingMeans.Destitute` —
the destitution penalty already charges the score for over-spending, and a floor is the
definition of discretionary. Budgeted at 12 blueprints/pass. Idempotent by construction: a laid
floor is `layerable` terrain and is skipped.

## #23 Surgery (`QueueLifesavingSurgery`, UpkeepModule)

The race, not the diagnosis: amputate only when `Severity/lethalSeverity >
Immunizable.Immunity`. Whole-body diseases (plague, malaria) have `hediff.Part == null` and
fall out naturally — for those, bed rest and cleanliness are all there is. Parts are filtered
by the game's own `BodyPartDef.canSuggestAmputation` (verified — excludes torso/head).

**The cleanliness gate is hard**: below room cleanliness 0 the bill is held and chronicled,
unless the disease is past 85% of lethal, where a dirty table beats a grave. `Cleaning` work
rises to 3.0 while anyone is losing, because the theatre floor is a medical instrument.

**Surprise that reshaped the design**: `InstallPegLeg` consumes **1 WoodLog directly** — there
is no peg-leg item to craft, so the entire prosthetic aftermath is a second bill. The ladder
above it (simple prosthetic ~3,900 research, bionic ~10,000) is deliberately not queued by
anything; a peg leg today, an upgrade when research pays the debt back.

Bills go on `pawn.health.surgeryBills` (verified field) as `Bill_Medical(recipe) + .Part`,
duplicate-checked — the same surface a player uses.

## #22 Growth (`GrowColonyGoal` + `plan.PopulationWanted`)

The missing top layer. Satisfied **unless** a spare built bed stands and `daysOfFood >= 6` —
the colony votes with its own larder on whether it can carry another mouth, which is the trap
guard: growing past food is how colonies die. It wants nothing buildable; its force runs
through levers that already existed as mechanics and never as intent:

- **IncidentModule**: joiner letters (matched on join/wanderer/refugee) are accepted when
  `PopulationWanted`, at `effectiveRisk >= 0.75` — but the existing food-crisis and danger
  discounts still apply, so a colony that wants people and cannot feed them still says no.
- **PrisonerModule**: `ColonistRecruitBias` gains +0.3 when `PopulationWanted` — a bias, not an
  override, and `PrisonerPolicy`'s own food check still holds.

## #15 Comfort (`ComfortGoal` + tea policy + production)

Psychite tea is the labour-free mood lever: **PsychoidBrewing 500, Neolithic, brewed at a
campfire or stove** (verified — not the drug lab it resembles), joy 0.40 at addictiveness
0.02, leaves from the social plot that already exists and is gated on `CanProcess`.

Three pieces: the goal (LongTerm, `Requires = ResearchCapacity`, urgency keyed on `minMood` —
the worst colonist, not the average — and only when fed); a production target of 2 cups/head
once brewable; and the consumption gate, which is the game's own machinery rather than a rule
of ours — **`DrugPolicyEntry.onlyIfMoodBelow = 0.35` with `allowedForJoy`** (verified field;
the default SocialDrugs policy covers beer and joints and *not* tea, so the entry must be
written). A content colonist never touches it; one at 0.34 has something between them and a
break. The AlcoholWithdrawal postmortem is the reason for the floor.

## #19 Herd growth (`EnsureFodderPlot` scaling)

Not fence surgery. The pen is sized once and widening a perimeter is high-risk placement churn;
the shortfall it creates is *forage in the lean season*, and the fodder plot is already the
answer to that. The plot now scales — `max(40, animals × 12)` cells — and **widens an existing
zone** by adding cells when the herd has grown past it, re-checked every pass against the herd
that exists now rather than the one the pen was fenced for.

## What was deliberately not done

- No direct `CompRefuelable`/`SetTerrain`/instant-spawn anything: every lever queues work a
  colonist performs. The refuel rule set this line; floors and surgery follow it.
- No bionic/prosthetic production chain: behind Electricity, which these colonies do not reach.
- No amputation for whole-body disease, no surgery in a dirty room short of imminent death.
- Goal arbitration verified by self-test after the goal-set change: all 24 probes unchanged,
  the two new goals surface in no scenario — they pull only when nothing nearer pulls harder.
